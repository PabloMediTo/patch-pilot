# Build bounded change and verification loop

## Objective

Turn reproduced issues into bounded plans and diffs, then verify and critique them with a visible, limited retry loop.

## Key Results

- [ ] Plans identify justified files, checks, and constraints before modification.
- [ ] Diffs stay within configured MVP limits.
- [ ] Verification and critique preserve evidence for every attempt and stop after the retry budget.
