# Enable human review and approval

## Objective

Give maintainers a trustworthy timeline and review surface for the exact plan, diff, evidence, attempts, and approval decision of a run.

## Key Results

- [ ] Historical timeline data survives reconnects and worker restarts.
- [ ] Live progress appears without polling the workflow engine.
- [ ] Approval is bound to the reviewed revision, diff hash, plan, and verification result.
